Employee home — Today-first mobile layout (24 November 2026)
============================================================

The employee home now presents attendance before schedule browsing. On mobile,
its order is compact workspace branding, greeting/date, today's attendance,
a 2-by-2 group of work shortcuts, then the schedule and selected-day details.
Desktop presents attendance/actions alongside the schedule.

Attendance
----------
The top card shows today's scheduled time before clock-in, a live elapsed timer
while working, or actual check-in/out times and worked duration after checkout.
Day-off, approved-leave, loading and error states are explicit. Active attendance
from a previous business day remains visible and can be clocked out.

Attendance reads and today's schedule do not depend on the calendar selection.
Browsing another week or month cannot change the shift ID submitted by clock-in.
Re-entry uses the completed attendance record's shift; legacy unlinked records
can resolve through that record's business date and today's schedule. Pending
requests are checked before allowing re-entry, and successful requests disable
repeat submission. Records that cannot be linked ask for a manager review.

Clock-out remains available during schedule errors and does not require GPS.
Completion is refreshed from the server; the client does not invent a checkout
timestamp. Existing GPS/geofence handling, swipe keyboard accessibility, haptic
feedback, shift locks, request approval and request cancellation are retained.
No operation is automatically replayed after a network failure.

Schedule and navigation
-----------------------
The schedule starts with a Monday-first week strip. Full month expands the same
schedule, including adjacent dates needed to fill complete weeks. Date selection
uses YYYY-MM-DD rather than a day number shared across different months. Leave
queries use overlapping start/end boundaries, not an OR that matches all leave.
Async read version checks prevent older month responses replacing newer results.

My tasks and Daily checklist open the existing employee pages; Report an issue
opens the existing incident form. More operations expands the other module links.
Tapping Home while already on Home scrolls back to the attendance card and moves
keyboard focus there. Existing bottom navigation and native keyboard handling
remain compatible.

Schedule wall-clock dates/hours retain the project's existing convention. Actual
attendance timestamps and the definition of today use Asia/Kolkata. This change
adds no tables, columns, migrations, routes or permission changes.
