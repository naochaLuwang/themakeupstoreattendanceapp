Full description:
THE MAKEUP STORE HRM is the staff workspace for THE MAKEUP STORE, Wangkhei.
It is intended for authorized employees, managers and administrators. A
store-issued account and an internet connection are required; there is no
public employee sign-up.

Employees can review shifts, record attendance, view payslips and leave records,
complete assigned tasks and daily checklists, and submit store reports with
supporting photos or selected incident audio files. Managers and administrators
review store work and attendance according to their account permissions.

Smart Scan reads the store attendance QR code using the camera. Foreground
location is used for attendance verification when requested. The app does not
continuously track location in the background. Work notifications are optional
and require Android notification permission and device registration.

Need access or help? Contact your store administrator or luwangtech0@gmail.com.

DATA SAFETY ASSESSMENT — REVIEW BEFORE SUBMITTING
Information transmitted off the device for app functionality includes:
- Personal information: employee/account names, account email or username and
  account/user IDs. Distinguish administrative email aliases from personal
  email addresses according to the actual deployed account setup.
- Location: precise coordinates and quality/time metadata for requested
  attendance verification. Required for the relevant on-site attendance flow,
  not continuous background collection. Check whether approximate-only data
  is accepted/transmitted before selecting that separate data type.
- Financial information: salary/payroll, worked hours, advances and payslips
  where these features are used; assess the Other financial information type.
  The app does not collect payment-card data or process consumer purchases.
- Photos: optional user-selected evidence uploaded for store reports.
- Audio: optional incident voice-note files selected from the device.
- Other user-generated content/app activity: report text, comments, tasks,
  checklist results, audit/incident entries and attendance activity.
- Device or other IDs: Firebase installation IDs, FCM tokens and the app's
  notification installation/device registration identifiers for opted-in push.
  Account for Firebase SDK behavior as well as custom application collection.
- Technical service logs: assess IP/delivery/security/diagnostic information
  processed by the hosting, authentication and messaging services. No Google
  Analytics or Crashlytics integration is claimed by this build.

Main purposes: app functionality, account management, support and security/
fraud prevention. Distinguish optional uploads/push from features that require
attendance or employment data. Do not declare that the app collects no data.
QR camera frames are decoded locally and are not uploaded as scan videos.
Photos explicitly attached to reports are uploaded, including before the final
report submission. PDF exports are downloads; do not label them as uploaded
documents unless a production feature actually uploads such documents.

Supabase, Hostinger and Firebase are operational processors. Check Google's
service-provider/user-initiated-transfer definitions and the actual agreements
before answering the third-party sharing questions; the presence of a provider
alone does not determine that answer. The app uses HTTPS in transit. Do not
claim end-to-end encryption, zero retention or automatic deletion deadlines.
The operator must establish and follow appropriate retention periods for
employment, payroll, audit and backup records and explain retained data when
handling individual requests. No blanket retention period was invented here.
