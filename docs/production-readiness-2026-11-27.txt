ATTENDANCE-TO-ADMIN NOTIFICATION FLOW
The private database trigger on completed attendance_operations creates alerts
for successful changed employee clock_in/clock_out operations. It covers manual
attendance, server actions and the Smart Scan engine receipt path. It ignores
failed attempts, unchanged requests, operation replays and administrator
corrections. A unique private event key deduplicates notification creation.
Global admins and admins assigned to the attendance branch are recipients;
notification preferences and device opt-in remain in force. A current admin
role and matching branch are rechecked before attendance-alert delivery.

Notifications use existing history and Realtime, include employee/branch/India
time in the authenticated app, and open /admin/logs. Lock-screen text remains
generic. Each alert has a separate high-priority FCM message/tag without the
reminder collapse key. After a successful response, Next.js after() wakes the
durable queue. Failures do not change the returned attendance result; scheduled
workers drain retries. Delivery is at least once, and OS/network restrictions
can delay it. Local provider tests mock FCM, not a physical phone.
